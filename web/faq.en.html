General
What does the app actually do?
It controls the PS5's fan according to a target temperature – calmly rather than hectically – and comes with an interface for games, packages, saved data, files, payloads, profile and system: in the browser or as a full-screen app with the controller.
Do I need internet?
No. The app goes onto the internet (GitHub) only when you trigger it: loading the payload list and payloads, or fetching newer versions of AMPR EMU NG, Dump Forge and Kraken Mount. The “Credits” page only checks whether github.com can be reached, so that the links become clickable.
Which firmware is supported?
Firmware 1.00 to 14.22; on other versions the app does not start. 12.00 is tested on a PS5 Pro (CFI-7021); users also report it working on 4.xx to 10.xx and 13.60.
What has to run on the console?
A jailbreak with an ELF loader (port 9021) and kstuff for fan control. ShadowMountPlus is optional, but it is needed for the tiles, mounting and unmounting, moving, unpacking and ejecting USB drives (chapter “Requirements”).
Does the app cost anything?
No. It is free software under the GNU GPL, version 3 or later.
Starting & using
Do I have to send the app again after every restart?
Not with a tile: FAKE86921 and FAKE86922 start the app themselves. Without a tile, yes, because the ELF only runs in memory. The settings are kept.
Which tile do I need: FAKE86921 or FAKE86922?
FAKE86921 is the full-screen app for the controller, FAKE86922 opens the web interface in the console's browser; both start the app themselves and can sit side by side. Copy the folders from the zips to /data/homebrew/; ShadowMountPlus creates the tiles. The old media tile (PSCC69690) only opens the page and does not start the app.
I copied new tile folders, but the old version is still running – why?
The tile replaces an older running app when you tap it, but not while a job is running (packing, copying, installing …): then open it again afterwards. If that does not help, send the new ELF once to port 9021 or restart the console.
How do I open the interface on a phone or PC?
Open http://<PS5-IP>:8086/ in the browser; the IP is in the notification after the start. Or scan the QR code on the System page with your phone.
How do I see the temperature without opening the page?
Press the controller's mute button twice quickly: the temperature and fan speed appear at the top left.
Which languages is the app available in?
German, English, Italian, Spanish, French, Russian and Swedish – in the browser at the top right, in the full-screen app via Options button → “Sprache / Language”. Console notifications on the TV and rare texts that are not translated yet stay German; please report such gaps.
How do I operate the full-screen app with the controller?
Touchpad button: sidebar; L1/R1: area, L2/R2: sub-tab; the X button confirms, the Circle button goes back; Square button: actions, Triangle button: “More”; Options button: menu. The bar at the bottom shows what the buttons do right now. “Quit app” closes only the full-screen app; fan control keeps running.
Why does the app show only part of my serial number?
The interface has no login; shown in full, every device on the home network could read the number. On the System page Full shows the whole number (full-screen app: X button on the row), Shortened switches back.
Fan & temperature
Which target temperature makes sense?
66 °C by default; up to about 72 °C is a good range for most people. Quick select sets Cool 62 °C, Balanced 77 °C or Quiet 91 °C. 91 °C is the console's own value: as quiet as without the app, but noticeably warmer.
How do I make the fan quieter?
On the Cooling page, set the target higher, choose the operating mode “Quiet” or lower the Fan speed ceiling; for the night only, Night mode helps. Quieter always means warmer; above the emergency limit no ceiling applies.
Can the app overheat my console?
Above the emergency limit the fan goes to full speed regardless of loudness, and the firmware always protects itself. In addition, the fan guard sets full cooling or hands the fan back to the console if the control hangs or it gets too hot anyway.
The console shows “Temperaturwarnung” – what does that mean?
The processor has reached “Warning from” (80 °C by default, 5 °C more for the main chip); with a high target the threshold is automatically at target + 2 °C. Make sure there is free airflow (dust, distance from the wall) or lower the target.
Why doesn't the fan spin up immediately?
On purpose: the control works with an average, lets the speed rise gently from 10 °C below the target and adjusts in small steps. How strongly it responds is set by the operating mode.
What happens when I quit the app?
Then the console controls the fan itself again. The same applies when “Automatic mode” is off.
The fan is “not available” – what can I do?
kstuff is not loaded. Load kstuff and send the app again.
Games, packages & saved data
Does deleting a game delete my saved data?
No, the saved data stays. Nothing is deleted until you press “Delete” in the window, which names what will go; the app never deletes a running game.
Which format should I choose when converting?
ffpfsc is compressed and recommended, ffpkg and exFAT are uncompressed. You only need FPKG for Kraken Mount. More in the chapter “Games”.
Is the original deleted when converting, copying or unpacking?
No, by default it stays (“Original afterwards: Keep”). If you choose “Delete” or “Move”, that only happens once the job has ended well; “Delete” asks first.
My game does not start after packing (LZ4 or zstd) – what can I do?
“Unpack LZ4 …” or “Unpack zstd …” brings all files back, checked. Then test the game unpacked with the same runtime (APR-EMU or AMPR EMU NG); if it does not run like that either, it does not tolerate that runtime (chapter “LZ4 asset packs: step by step”).
LZ4 or zstd – which should I choose?
LZ4 is read by APR-EMU and AMPR EMU NG; only the game data is packed. zstd is read only by AMPR EMU NG, packs almost the whole game and usually saves more space. If a game runs with LZ4, there is no reason to switch. When converting to exFAT or ffpkg you can pack right away (chapter “Games”).
A large package does not fit on my FAT32 stick.
FAT32 allows at most 4 GB per file. Under “Packages”, “Split…” cuts the package into fitting parts; the original stays.
An installation was aborted – where is my storage space?
The console reserves it right away. Remove the aborted entry in its download list with “Delete”.
Where are my saved data backups, and can I take them to another console?
On the chosen drive under PS5-Sicherung/Spielstaende/<Datum>; these backups belong to their console. To transfer, press “As ZIP” at the title and choose the file on the other console under “Import saved data” (chapter “Back up saved data”). The ZIP file is not encrypted.
Backing up, restoring or “As ZIP” does not work because a game is running – what to do?
The app only touches saved data when no game is running – not even a paused one, because it can still keep the saved data open. Close the game (“Close game” on its card) and try again.
Can the app copy discs or dump disc games?
The Disc tab makes an ISO of a PS2 DVD or PS3 disc; PS4 and PS5 discs it only recognises. A game installed from disc is dumped by “Start dumping” under “Dump disc games”: the app starts it and dumps it after the load time. Only for backups of your own games.
My PS4 games from USB or my Kraken Mount games do not appear – why?
“PS4 PKG from USB” (PS4Mount) and “KrakenMount” (Kraken Mount) are off by default. Press Switch on in the tab on the Games page; the app remembers the choice and from then on starts them every time it starts.
Does Kraken Mount also work without ShadowMountPlus?
Yes. If Kraken Mount is switched on and no ShadowMountPlus is running, the app also gives it homebrew, etaHEN/games and the main folders of the drives after a minute; it registers the games there itself. When ShadowMountPlus starts, the app takes the folders out again within a few seconds. The lamp in the KrakenMount tab shows the state.
File manager
Why can't I change some folders?
Only /, /dev, /proc, links, mounted drives as a whole and the folders directly under / and /mnt themselves are locked – their contents can still be changed. Whatever the console has mounted read-only, it refuses by itself. Take care under /system_data, /user and in the app's folder.
Is anything overwritten when copying?
Only if you want it to be: with the same name, a window asks for “Replace”, “Keep both”, “Skip” or “Cancel”. “Cancel” during copying removes the entry that was started in the target again.
Can I view or edit files without downloading them?
Yes: “View” shows pictures and the beginning of text files (up to 256 KB), “Edit” opens text files up to 64 KB in an editor. Download larger files, edit them and upload them again.
Can I sort and search for files?
Yes: sort by name, size or date above the list. The “Search” field filters the open folder (with * and ? as wildcards), “Include subfolders” searches the whole drive. Full-screen app: Triangle button → “Search …”.
How do I eject a USB stick safely?
Press Eject next to the drive among the locations or under System → Storage (full-screen app: Square button on the drive). This needs ShadowMountPlus 1.7beta10 or later and does not work while a job of the app or a game is using the drive. Only unplug once “… can be unplugged” appears; the console picks up some sticks again by itself afterwards.
Can the app read and write Windows NTFS drives?
Yes, in the file manager as ntfs0: uploading, new folders, copying, moving and deleting work, renaming, editing and replacing do not; games cannot be started from it. If “Read-only” appears, the note says what to do on the Windows PC. Use “Eject” before unplugging.
Problems & security
The app does not start or the page cannot be reached – what can I do?
Check the firmware (1.00 to 14.22, otherwise the app does not start at all), send the ELF to port 9021 again and open http://<PS5-IP>:8086/. If the console reports that port 8086 is held by an older instance, close the old one under Payloads → “Quit” or restart. After a network change the app reopens the access by itself after about ten seconds.
“Device busy” appears when tapping a tile or starting a game – what can I do?
Usually tapping again helps; for this the app pauses a game in the background by itself (Cooling → “Pause the game in the background”, on by default) and starts a refused tile once more by itself after a few seconds. A game that is only mounted but not started blocks all other starts: start it or press Unmount on it (a game the app mounted itself is released by the app after 30 seconds).
“The drive has disconnected (Device not configured)”
Almost always the cable, the port or too little power. Try a different, short cable directly on the console, and restart the console if problems persist.
I get error 403.
The app refuses requests from foreign websites and only accepts the console's IP address, localhost or names with a home-network ending (.local, .lan, .home …). Open the interface via the IP address.
Is the interface password-protected?
No, every device on the home network can use it – so do not set up port forwarding. If you want, set the bind address to 127.0.0.1 (“Local only”). ShadowMountPlus, Kraken Mount and Dump Forge have their own ports without a login (chapter “Security on the home network”).
The page stutters when scrolling in the PS5 browser.
Choose the design “Optimal”: it is the fastest to draw.
Is this legal?
The app contains no games, no firmware, no foreign keys (only a publicly known one for self-built test packages) and nothing that circumvents copy protection. Modifying a console may violate terms of use; whoever does it bears the risk. Piracy is not supported.
