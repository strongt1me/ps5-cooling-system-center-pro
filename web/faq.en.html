General
What does the app actually do?
It controls the PS5's fan according to a target temperature – calm and slow rather than loud and hectic – and offers a web interface for games, packages, saved data, files, payloads, profile and system information.
Do I need internet?
No. The app only connects to programs on the console and to the browsers on your home network. The only exception, in the browser: the “Credits” page checks once whether github.com is reachable, so that the links become clickable.
Which firmware is supported?
Firmware 12.00 on a PS5 Pro (CFI-7021) is tested. The app is built with an SDK that goes up to 13.60; on 13.xx, however, the app has not been tested.
Does the app cost anything?
No. It is free software under the GNU GPL, version 3 or later.
Starting & using
Do I have to send the app again after every restart?
Yes. The ELF runs in memory. Send it to port 9021 once after every restart of the console. The settings are kept.
The tile only opens an empty page – why?
The tile does not start the app; it only opens the web interface. If the app has not been sent yet after a restart, there is nothing to show.
How do I open the interface on a phone or PC?
Open http://<PS5-IP>:8086/ in the browser. The IP is in the notification on the TV after the start.
Can I start the app twice?
Please don't: two instances control the fan at the same time and interfere with each other. Stop an old instance on the “Payloads” page or restart the console.
How do I see the temperature without opening the page?
Press the controller's mute button twice quickly: the temperature and fan speed appear at the top right.
Why doesn't the page move when a window is open?
That is on purpose: while a window is open, the background stays still, and the right stick only scrolls the window itself.
In which languages is the interface available?
German, English, Italian, Spanish, French and Russian. You choose the language at the top right of the header; the manual and the FAQ are available in all six. Notifications that the console shows on the TV stay in German.
A text is still in German – is that a bug?
Not a bug in the app, but a gap: rare texts and console messages are not translated into every language and then appear in German. Please report them; the dictionaries are easy to extend.
Fan & temperature
Which target temperature makes sense?
66 °C by default. Cool = 62 °C, Balanced = 77 °C, Quiet = 91 °C. Higher means quieter and warmer; up to 72 °C is a good range for most people. 91 °C is the value the console sets itself: as quiet as without the app, but the console gets noticeably warmer; the warning threshold moves with it automatically (target + 2 °C).
Can the app overheat my console?
The app only holds the temperature you set. Above a safety limit, only the hardware counts, and the PS5's firmware always protects itself independently of that.
Why doesn't the fan spin up immediately?
The control is deliberately slow: it works with an average, ignores small fluctuations of ±1 °C and adjusts in small steps.
What happens when I quit the app?
Then the console controls the fan itself again according to its normal settings. The same applies when “Automatic mode” is switched off.
The fan is “not available” – what can I do?
kstuff is not loaded. Without kstuff, the app cannot reach the fan controller. Load kstuff and send the app again.
Games, packages & saved data
Does deleting a game delete my saved data?
No. Saved data is always kept. Deleting only happens after ticking the box and clicking twice.
Which format should I choose when converting?
ffpfsc is compressed and recommended. ffpkg and exFAT are uncompressed. Please recreate older .ffpkg files made with versions before 1.48.0.
A large package does not fit on my FAT32 stick.
FAT32 allows at most 4 GB per file. Under “Packages”, “Split…” cuts a package into suitable parts – the original stays unchanged.
An installation was aborted – where is my storage space?
The console reserves the space immediately. Remove the aborted entry from the console's download list with “Delete”.
Where are my saved data backups?
On the drive you choose when backing up, in the folder PS5-Sicherung/Spielstaende/<Datum> – on the internal SSD under /data.
Can I restore saved data from another console?
No. Saved data is tied to its console; such backups can only be verified.
File manager
Why can't I change some folders?
Changes are only possible on the drives and in /data. System folders are for viewing only – this protects the console.
Why is the app's folder locked?
Settings and logs are stored there. Only the subfolder payloads can be changed, so that you can upload payloads.
Is anything overwritten when copying?
No, never. If a name already exists in the destination, nothing starts, and the message names it.
What happens with “Cancel”?
The operation stops; whatever of the started entry had already been written to the destination is removed again.
How do I view a file without downloading it?
With “View” on the file: pictures (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, BMP, ICO) appear in a window; for text files, the beginning up to 256 KB. Binary files can only be downloaded.
Can I sort the list by size or date?
Yes: above the list you choose “Name”, “Size” or “Date”; the arrow reverses the order. With “Folder sizes”, the console also counts the size of the folders.
Problems & security
“The drive has disconnected (Device not configured)”
Almost always the USB cable, the port or too little power. Try a different, short cable directly on the console; if there are problems afterwards, restart the console.
I get error 403.
The app refuses requests that come from outside websites. Open the interface directly via the console's IP address.
Is the interface password-protected?
No. Every device on the home network can use it. So do not set up port forwarding; if you like, set the network binding to 127.0.0.1.
Unknown folders with zip files in /data/homebrew?
They usually come from another tool with automatic saved data backup (e.g. ProsperoMgr), not from this app.
Is this legal?
The app contains no games, no firmware, no keys and nothing that circumvents copy protection. Modifying a console may violate terms of use; whoever does it bears the risk. Piracy is not supported.
